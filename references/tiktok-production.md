# TikTok Production Guidance

## Product evidence

Use one real product reference as the identity authority. Preserve exact labels, colors, countable parts, attachments, and shown sides. A product photo establishes appearance, not performance claims. Phrase claims exactly as supplied or make them visibly demonstrable.

## Faceless visual direction

- Keep any person to hands, forearms, a cropped body, or first-person point of view.
- Use one consistent hand appearance, sleeve, setting, and lighting direction throughout a video.
- Let a hand cause every product movement; without a hand, keep the object fixed and move only the camera slightly.
- Prefer clean phone footage: natural or practical light, readable focus, real surface texture, restrained color, and plausible contact shadows.
- Do not show a face, a mouth, a mirror reflection, unsupported product sides, duplicate products, or text baked into generated video.

## Script and shot pacing

Use an action or result immediately—no greeting, logo sting, or static product reveal. Keep each spoken line tied to a visible feature, action, or result. Change the visual action or framing every few seconds when it helps comprehension, rather than cutting for its own sake.

For each shot, specify:

`time range · framing · action · visible endpoint · continuity requirements`

Example: `0–3s · macro close-up · one hand opens the latch · lid is visibly open · same tan sleeve, kitchen counter, daylight from left.`

## Captions and disclosure

Add captions during editing, keeping the bottom portion clear of essential product details. If the video is sponsored, affiliate-linked, or otherwise requires disclosure, use a conspicuous on-screen disclosure and follow the user's applicable platform and legal requirements. Do not imply a purchase, review, or outcome that the user did not substantiate.

## Acceptance check

- Vertical 9:16 export unless the user chose another format.
- Product is identifiable and unchanged from its real reference.
- No face or speaking mouth is visible.
- The hook, use demonstration, proof point, and final payoff are all present.
- Audio and captions match the approved script.
- No unsupported promises, brand substitutions, or accidental on-video text.
